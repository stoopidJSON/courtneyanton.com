My story

Hello, and thank you for visiting my site! My name is Courtney and I am a mother of two lovely, beautiful daughters. Early in my life I discovered that I had a passion for photography, and the birth of my daughters deepened that passion. It was a joy to capture their special moments, and it often makes my heart ache to revisit those moments years later. Photography is a craft unlike any other, and it is my goal to share my skill with you and to help capture your family's moments.

Services

Newborn

Make the gift of life last forever with professional and personal photographs of your new bundle of joy. With the right lens and the right angle, we can craft art that you won’t be able to take your eyes off of.

Baby

They truly do grow up far too fast, don't they? For their first birthday--or really any milestone--it’s important to pause time just for a moment to capture the beauty and make it last forever.

Child

Curious, creative, loving: our children are already an undeniable and precious work of art – we’re just here to celebrate it and create a timeless memory for the whole family to cherish forever.

Maternity

Just because you haven’t met them yet, doesn’t mean you don’t love them unconditionally – and vice versa! All you need to do is bring your bump and enthusiasm. Let’s make a shoot to remember!

Family

Calling all Moms, Dads, Grandparents, Kids, and more. There is great power in numbers, and the more the merrier around these parts! So gather the troops and let’s make some memories.
